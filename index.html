How to Play

On your turn, do exactly one action: draw Train Car cards, claim one route, or draw Destination Tickets.

To claim a colored route, spend the same number of matching cards as the route length. Gray routes may use any single color. Locomotives are wild.

Completed Destination Tickets add their printed value at game end; incomplete ones subtract it. Route points: 1→1, 2→2, 3→4, 4→7, 5→10, 6→15.
