How to Play

🟢 GREEN — Same Order
Say: dog → cat → lion
🔴 RED — Reverse Order
Say: lion → cat → dog

Watch the circles appear one by one in random positions. They stay on the board. When the sequence is complete, the microphone turns on. Say each emoji name one at a time, or tap the circles.

Tap an item to hear its English name. These words match your selected category and difficulty.
