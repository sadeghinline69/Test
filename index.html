📘 How to Play

Memorize four colors and their directions.

↓

When a colored ball flies across the screen, tap it or say its color to stop it. Then say its remembered direction: up, down, left, or right.

Also accepted: upward/upwards/above/go up; downward/downwards/below/go down; to the left/go left/move left; to the right/go right/move right.

Correct direction = +1 point.
